How to read the ablations. receptor_only and peptide_only are given ONE side of the pair and cannot know which partner is being scored, so whatever they achieve is the artifact floor set by the sampling rather than learned pairing. The full model only demonstrates pairing to the extent it beats BOTH of them. no_interaction drops the element-wise product, isolating how much the interaction term contributes.
